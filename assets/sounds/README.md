# Radial sound styles

Original synthesized PCM audio created for Tablet Pro Radial. These are the exact five styles approved from the subtle ASMR audition: 01 Felt click, 02 Walnut tap, 03 Bamboo ticks, 04 Tiny gears, and 10 Crystal whisper (default).

Each style contains press, opening, and closing clips. All files are mono, 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV. Playback gain is 0.45, matching the audition; hover ticks use 0.24. No third-party samples or field recordings are included. Clips have bounded peaks and faded endpoints. Sound on/off remains independent of the selected style.
